# About that Matt Shumer post that has nearly 50 million views

Gary Marcus — Feb 11, 2026
Source: https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/about-that-matt-shumer-post-that

Something Big is Happening, or so a lot of people want to believe.

All morning people have been asking me about a blog post by Matt Shumer that
has gone viral. It is a masterpiece of hype, written in the style of old
direct marketing campaigns, with bold-faced call outs like “I know this is
real because it happened to me first” and “I am no longer needed for the
actual technical work of my job”.

As told to a journalist:

> Shumer’s blog post is weaponized hype that tells people what they want to
> hear, but stumbles on the facts, especially with respect to reliability. He
> gives no actual data to support this claim that the latest coding systems
> can write whole complex apps without making errors. Similarly, when he
> describes how AIs are doing longer and longer tasks on METR’s famous
> task-time benchmark, he neglects to say the criterion on that benchmark is
> 50% correct, not 100%, and that the benchmark is only about coding and not
> tasks in general. No AI system can reliably do every five-hour long task
> humans can do without error, or even close.

Further points from Marcus:

1. Shumer ignored a different METR study where coders sometimes imagined
   productivity gains while actually losing productivity.
2. Other users do not report “it’s usually perfect.”
3. Autogenerated code security is in question.
4. Something has changed recently — you can sometimes give a description and
   let the system go — but that also makes over-trust more dangerous.
